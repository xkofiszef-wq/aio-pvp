## Changes since 1.0.53

### New
- **Reach Display (HUD):** shows the distance to the target on your last hit, from your eyes to its hitbox (the way the server measures it). The distance is taken at the moment you attack and shown only after the server confirms the hit, so misses don't count. It hides a few seconds after the hit (the "Show for" setting, 1-10 s). It works on mobs too, so you can practice on them. Move it in the HUD Editor like any other element.
